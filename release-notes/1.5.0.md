## Tidepool 1.5, the first public version

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

On iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. On Android, tap **Install** when your browser offers it. It updates by itself whenever a new version comes out.

### Halloween event: ends November 1

The Halloween shop is open for a limited time. Spend shells on four exotic skins that won't come back once the event ends:

- **Haunted harbor** (water), 100 shells
- **Jack-o'-lantern** (reef colors), 150 shells
- **Spooky spirits** (tap effect), 150 shells
- **Lantern phantom** (companion), 250 shells

### Season 1: Brinelight, ends November 21

The first season pass is live. Earn XP by playing, finishing missions, and earning trophies, then climb 100 tiers of rewards, including skins and shells.

### Windows

Download `Tidepool_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
