## Tidepool 1.6.1: the Tide market

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### New: the Tide market

A rotating skin shop in the Shop tab. It stocks 5 skins for shells, with at most one legendary at a time, and restocks every 5 hours. There are 12 new skins to find:

- **Water:** Tropic, Ripple bay, Plankton bloom
- **Reef colors:** Sunset, Berry dots, Aurora glass
- **Tap effect:** Raindrops, Confetti, Prism burst
- **Companion:** Little fish, Seahorse, Lantern angler

Once Season 1 ends, its common, rare and legendary pass skins join the market too.

### Skins

- The **Skins** button at the top of the screen now opens your skin inventory. The Shop only shows what is for sale.
- Skins you don't own yet stay hidden, except trophy skins. Tap **Show hidden skins** to see the rest.
- New **Event** rarity, with a red and white wavy border, for limited-time skins like the Halloween set.
- Tap effects and companions now show a picture of themselves instead of a solid color.
- Every reef and tide shows which ascension unlocks it.

### Still on: Halloween event (until November 1) and Season 1: Brinelight (until November 21)
