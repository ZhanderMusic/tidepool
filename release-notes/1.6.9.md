## Tidepool 1.6.9: pearls cost more, reefs give XP

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Pearl balance

Pearls were coming too easily once you were a few ascensions in. The plankton you need per pearl now grows with every ascension, so earning an ascension's pearls means you have worked through that tier's reefs and tides.

- **Ascension 1:** about 7x harder to earn pearls.
- **Ascension 2 and up:** much harder. You will earn most of your pearls in the last stretch of a tier.
- Pearls and pearl bonuses you already have are kept. Only new spawns use the new cost.

### Pass XP

Every reef you buy now gives 0.25 XP. Tides and upgrades still give 1 XP each.

### Still on: Halloween event (until November 1) and Season 1: Brinelight (until November 21)
