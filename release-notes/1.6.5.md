## Tidepool 1.6.5: ranks, crystals and app themes

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### New: Tide crystals

Finish ascension 3 and a new resource unlocks. Tide crystals trickle in while you play (even while you're away), and every ascension pays a bonus lump on top. They are never reset, and you spend them on five permanent upgrades: Tide resonance, Crystal bloom, Pearl facets, Prism grip and Deep echo.

### New: app themes

Five exotic skins that recolor the whole app, unlocked by reaching ascensions 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10: Reefglow, Stormcell, Starlit deep, Silver mirror and Sovereign gold. Equip them from the Skins button.

### Animated ranks

Your current rank and every step of the Ascension path now have their own unique animation. Tap any rank to read its story.

### Season 1: Brinelight

The season card has a drifting-lantern animation, and tapping it opens the story of where the Brinelight comes from.

### Settings and codes

A new cog next to the Skins button opens Settings: version, stats, save data and cloud save, erase, and a box to redeem codes for rewards. Trophies now shows only trophies.

### Still on: Halloween event (until November 1) and Season 1: Brinelight (until November 21)

### Windows

Download `Tidepool_1.6.5_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
