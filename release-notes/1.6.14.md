## Tidepool 1.6.14: pearls cost more from ascension 3

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Pearl balance

From ascension 3 on, earning pearls was too easy: one spawn could pay about twice what the whole ascension needs. Pearls now need about 75 of that tier's last reef instead of 25, so an ascension takes several spawns.

- Ascensions 0 to 2 are unchanged.
- Pearls and pearl bonuses you already have are kept. Only new spawns use the new cost.
- Each pearl still adds 5% production, so later spawns in an ascension come faster as you build up pearls.
