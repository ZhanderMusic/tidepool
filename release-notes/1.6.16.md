## Tidepool 1.6.16: lasting reefs and bigger ascensions (major update)

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Lasting reefs and tides

Tide crystals now buy upgrades that keep your work.

- **Lasting reefs:** one upgrade per group of reefs (Base, then Ascension 1, 2 and so on). Reefs in an upgraded group are kept when you spawn and when you ascend.
- **Lasting tides:** the same for tide upgrades, one per group.
- Each upgrade costs crystals, starting at 400 for Base and doubling for every group after it.
- Tide crystals unlock at ascension 3, and an upgrade shows once you have unlocked its group.

### Ascensions

Every ascension now needs double its original pearls (600 for the first, up to 30M for the last), instead of the 50% more from 1.6.15. A spawn can still pay at most 15% of that, and pearl upgrades raise the cap.

### Season 1

Season 1: Brinelight now ends on November 1.

A new animated splash plays the first time you open this major update. You can replay it from Settings, Patch notes.
