## Tidepool 1.6.8: harder ascensions

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Ascension balance

Ascensions now ask for more pearls, so you work through more of your reefs and tides before moving up. The early ones are the biggest change.

| Ascension | Pearls needed |
|---|---|
| 1 | 300 |
| 2 | 1,500 |
| 3 | 5,000 |
| 4 | 18,000 |
| 5 | 60,000 |
| 6 | 180,000 |
| 7 | 550,000 |
| 8 | 1.8M |
| 9 | 5M |
| 10 | 15M |

Ranks you have already earned are kept. If you were about to ascend and are now short, keep spawning to earn the rest.
