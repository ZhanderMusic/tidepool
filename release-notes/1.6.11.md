## Tidepool 1.6.11: Treasure chests (major update)

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Treasure

A new **Treasure** tab in the Shop. Spend **Doubloons** to pick one of three chests. All three have the same odds.

- **Earn Doubloons:** 1 per mission, 3 from the daily catch, 15 per ascension, 3 on every 5th pass tier up to tier 100, and 10 on the bonus tiers (105, 110, 125, 150). Everyone starts with 5. Players who are already far along get Doubloons for the tiers and ascensions they earned before.
- **Prizes:** shells, pass XP, XP boosts, pearls, Tide crystals (only after ascension 3), small permanent production boosts (+1% each, up to +25%), legendary vouchers and 8 new exotic skins.
- **Pity:** your 25th chest without an exotic is a guaranteed exotic. Skins you own never drop again.
- **New exotic skins:** Treasure grotto and Ghost galleon (water), Gemstone (reef colors), Gold coins (tap effect), Baby kraken and Treasure crab (companions), and the Doubloon night and Crimson tide app themes.

### Also since 1.6.10

- Pearls cost more at every ascension. You now need about 25 of a tier's last reef to earn that ascension's pearls.
- Each reef shows a progress bar to its next doubling and the total plankton per second it makes. Reefs are less cluttered.
- The Tide market restocks every 10 minutes and has 10 new skins.
- Settings has a Patch notes button and a Check for updates button.
- Major updates now play an animation the first time you open the game after updating.

### Still on: Halloween event (until November 1) and Season 1: Brinelight (until November 21)
