## Tidepool 1.6.13: a tidier reef and a prettier Track

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Reef scene

The reefs on the water are now arranged in three depth rows. The front row sits on the sand, and the two back rows are higher, smaller and a little dimmer, each standing on its own faint dune. Every reef type keeps its own stretch of the floor, so it no longer piles up in one spot.

### Pass Track

Rewards now show icons instead of text. Shells are a shell, Doubloons are a coin, and a skin reward shows a preview of the skin with its name, category and rarity. The big bonus tiers get larger icons.

### Sort by ascension (1.6.12)

Reef and Tide have a new row of tabs under the buy buttons. Pick All, Base or any ascension you have reached to see just those reefs and tide upgrades.
