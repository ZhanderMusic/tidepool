## Tidepool 1.6.15: a new ascension system (major update)

**▶ Play now or install on your phone:** https://zhandermusic.github.io/tidepool/

### Pearls and ascension

Pearls were too easy to stack up once you had a lot of upgrades. Pearls are now limited per spawn.

- A spawn can pay at most 15% of the pearls you need for your current ascension.
- Each level of Pearl luster, Pearl polish and Pearl facets adds 1% to that limit, up to 50% with all 35 levels.
- Every ascension now needs 50% more pearls.
- The Spawn tab shows the limit and tells you when you reach it.
- Pearls and pearl bonuses you already have are kept.

### Shop and skins

- The Shop skins, the Halloween shop and the treasure chests are now square cards. The skin rows scroll sideways.
- Skins you do not own yet are all in the hidden list, including trophy and ascension skins.
- A new animated splash plays the first time you open this major update. You can replay it from Settings, Patch notes.

### Still on: Halloween event (until November 1) and Season 1: Brinelight (until November 21)
